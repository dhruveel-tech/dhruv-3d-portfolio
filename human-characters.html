Rice Farmer — Low Poly Character for AR Experience

APPROX. 12,000 TRIANGLES · REAL-TIME / AR · PBR

A stylized rice farmer character created for an augmented reality (AR) experience. The goal was to design a lightweight and optimized character suitable for real-time applications while maintaining appealing visual details. Modeled in Blender and textured in Substance 3D Painter using a PBR workflow. Special attention was given to efficient topology and clean textures for smooth AR performance. Maps: Base Color, Roughness, Normal and AO.

Strixa — Neon Alley Walk

3D GAME CHARACTER · BLENDER CYCLES · SUBSTANCE PAINTER

Strixa blends elegance and mystery in a futuristic street setting. Modeling and posing were completed in Blender, with texturing in Substance Painter to achieve semi-translucent fabric and realistic skin shading. The outfit uses minimal yet intricate line work with a fashion-forward sci-fi aesthetic. The neon alley scene uses atmospheric lighting, signs and reflections to emphasize the character. The environment model was sourced from Sketchfab for presentation purposes.
