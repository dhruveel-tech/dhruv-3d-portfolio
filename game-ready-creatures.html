CREATURE ART · GAME ASSETS

Creatures &
Beasts.

Two collections of game creature work: Game-Ready Creatures and Gate of Abyss — Creatures. The work focuses on strong silhouettes, readable forms, expressive details, optimized geometry and material definition. Models were created in Blender and textured in Substance 3D Painter.

Cerberus Dragon — Bioluminescent Hydra Beast

19,000 TRIANGLES · GAME CREATURE

Crow

Muscimol Octopus

6,000 TRIANGLES · LOW-POLY CREATURE

Mushroom Cat — Whimsical Stylized Character

A cheerful cat character in a cozy fantasy mushroom world. A custom apron with coffee-themed patches and a scarf adds personality, while oversized mushrooms create a warm presentation setting.

06 — GATE OF ABYSS · TIPSY COMPANY

Gate of Abyss — Creatures.

Five creature characters created for Gate of Abyss by Tipsy Company. Modeled in Blender and textured in Substance Painter.

Vizgorr the Arcane Seer

7,816 TRIANGLES · DARK FANTASY CREATURE

A grotesque flying creature with a single expressive eye, a hooked wizard-like silhouette and wide membranous wings, finished with detailed surface textures and glowing accents.

The Beast — Molten Tiger

17,000 TRIANGLES · GAME CREATURE

A powerful tiger-like beast infused with molten energy. Dark sculpted forms and fiery emissive cracks create a threatening fantasy-game presence.

Rutiny — The Owl Guardian

8,766 TRIANGLES · STYLIZED CREATURE

An owl-inspired guardian with expressive eyes, layered feather forms and a heroic silhouette, balancing stylized proportions with game-friendly geometry.

Hedgehog

7,500 TRIANGLES · STYLIZED CREATURE

A compact fantasy creature with a rounded silhouette, prominent eyes and textured spines, designed to read clearly as a stylized game character.

Penguin

8,000 TRIANGLES · STYLIZED CREATURE

A charming hooded penguin character with a strong color palette, expressive face and costume details.
