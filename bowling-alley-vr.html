PROJECT OVERVIEW

Two themes, one experience.

This project showcases two themed environments developed for a VR bowling experience. My focus was on building the 3D environment and its visual presentation, including scene layout, modular props, furniture, decorative assets, materials and lighting.

All presented environment renders were created in Blender. The two themes were designed to feel distinct while maintaining a consistent bowling experience.

MY CONTRIBUTION & TOOLS

3D environment art.

  • Environment and prop modeling in Blender.
  • Scene layout, composition and material assignment.
  • UV unwrapping and PBR texturing in Substance 3D Painter.
  • Lighting and final environment rendering in Blender.

Software: Blender, Substance 3D Painter.

Development credit: Unity implementation and Meta Quest integration were handled by the development team. My contribution was the 3D environment art and Blender renders.
