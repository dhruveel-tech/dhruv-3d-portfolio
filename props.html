Stylized Battle Axe — Game Ready Weapon Asset

3D GAME ASSET · BLENDER · SUBSTANCE 3D PAINTER

A stylized battle axe designed for games and fantasy-themed experiences, blending medieval weapon forms with futuristic material accents. Modeled in Blender with attention to silhouette and proportion, UV unwrapped, and textured using a PBR workflow in Substance Painter. Presented with contrasting metallic surfaces and detailed handle elements.

Horned Steel — Warrior’s Helm

GAME ASSET · LOW-POLY · BLENDER · SUBSTANCE 3D PAINTER

A stylized warrior helmet with an intimidating silhouette, bold curved horns and a rugged steel finish. Modeled in Blender, then baked and textured in Substance Painter. The asset focuses on optimized topology, clean UVs and a combination of metallic surfaces and twisted horn forms for fantasy RPGs or stylized animation.

Character Outfit

GAME ASSET · BLENDER · LOW-POLY SCULPT DETAILING

A detailed fantasy character outfit created as a standalone game asset. The garment set combines layered clothing, padded sleeves, belts, straps, pouches and attached equipment. Modeled in Blender, with sculpted surface details developed directly on the low-poly workflow and prepared through a baking-focused presentation. The work emphasizes garment construction, layered forms and readable detail for real-time character use.

Game-Ready Padded Leather Jacket

GAME-READY CLOTHING ASSET · BLENDER · SUBSTANCE 3D PAINTER

A stylized low-poly padded leather jacket designed with efficient topology and a realistic material finish. The base mesh and high-poly surface details were created in Blender, with texturing and baking in Substance Painter. Custom roughness, leather bump and ambient-occlusion detail add depth while keeping the asset suitable for real-time use.

Immersive Low-Poly Jungle Environment for VR

3D ENVIRONMENT · UNITY · BLENDER · META QUEST / PC VR

A stylized low-poly jungle environment developed for an immersive VR experience. Modular assets such as trees, terrain, rocks and foliage were created in Blender, with scene setup in Unity. The environment workflow focuses on optimized geometry, lighting and shaders for VR performance, supported by ambient sound and dynamic lighting.
